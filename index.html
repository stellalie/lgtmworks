// about

For dev, by dev

The name says it: LGTM, "looks good to me", the two best words in code review. That's the bar for everything we make.

Developer tools got heavier while AI made the work faster. We think the tools around your agents should be small, quick and out of your way. So we make little ones, one at a time, from Sydney.
